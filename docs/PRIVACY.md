# Privacy notice draft

This is the plain language privacy notice for Brainwave Quest phase 1.

## What the game collects

Phase 1 has no accounts and no sign in. The game collects:

- A nickname chosen by the child.
- An avatar choice.
- Game progress stored in the browser.
- Reports sent through the Report a mistake button.

The game does not collect real names, photos, schools, locations or emails during solo play.

## What the game does not do

- No ads.
- No third party trackers.
- No open chat.
- No friend requests.
- No purchases.
- No accounts for players.
- No AI calls from the live game.

## Read aloud

Read aloud uses the browser built in speech. No audio leaves the device.

## Legal review

This draft needs review by a Nigerian lawyer before launch. The review must cover the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the COPPA rule amendments as they apply to a Nigerian pilot.

## Phase 1 limits

Phase 1 has no parent accounts and no child profile consent records. Those features arrive in phase 2.

## Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to the owner through GitHub.
